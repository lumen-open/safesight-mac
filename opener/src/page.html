Opened

Your Mac's admin password

    Keep it private. Whoever has this password can use your Mac as an administrator until SafeSight Mac replaces it. This page keeps no copy.

    What to do next

    1. Start your Mac.
    2. At the login window, choose the account named SafeSight Mac administrator and type this password. If it isn't in the list, choose Other… and type that name.
    3. The SafeSight Mac app will offer Finish leaving and Stay.
    4. You have 7 days from the Mac's first start to choose. After that, this password stops working and SafeSight Mac goes on protecting the Mac.

    Done

    Cleared

    The password is gone from this page. You can close it now. If you need it again, open your recovery copy again with your Recovery Key.
