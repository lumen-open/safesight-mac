Terms and risks

Version 0.1, draft, 19 November 2026. This version covers the early preview, which has the screen cover but not the lock yet. It hasn’t been read by a lawyer.

SafeSight Mac is free, open-source software. Please read this short page before you install it.

Who it’s for

  • Adults.

    It’s for people aged 18 or over. The app can’t check your age, so we’re trusting you.

  • Your own Mac.

    Install it only on a Mac you own and control. Not a work or school Mac, and not one you share with someone else.

  • Your own choice.

    Set it up for yourself, never for someone else, and never make it a condition of a job, a home, a membership or anyone’s support. If someone else controls your password, they control your Mac, and no software can change that.

What it is, and what it isn’t

SafeSight Mac covers explicit images on your screen. It’s a tool, not a treatment for anything, and it doesn’t replace the people in your life or professional help.

What can go wrong

  • It can miss an explicit image, and it sometimes covers a harmless one. How many it misses hasn’t been measured yet.
  • An image can show for a moment before its cover appears.
  • Protected video, like films in streaming apps, can’t be checked.
  • It only protects this Mac, not your phone, a TV or another computer.
  • This preview has no lock, so you can pause or quit it at any time.
  • It’s an early release built with the help of AI, and nobody independent has reviewed it yet. It may have bugs, so keep a backup of your Mac.

The full list of known limits is in docs/STEP-1-STATUS.md.

No warranty

SafeSight Mac is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind, by an open-source project, not a company. The licence (Apache 2.0) has the full wording, including its limits on liability.

There’s no support line. Bugs go in the public issue tracker, and anything that could help someone get around SafeSight Mac goes privately through SECURITY.md.

When this page changes

It only changes with a new release, and the version and date at the top change with it.
