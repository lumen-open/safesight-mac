Free, open-source blocking of explicit images on your Mac. Once it’s on, it stays on.

SafeSight Mac watches your screen and covers explicit images as soon as they appear: in any app, in any browser, on every display. Everything else is left alone. Your work, your calls, your music and your code carry on as normal.

How it stays on

What makes it different is that it stays on.

There’s no off switch for a bad night, no password to beg for, and nobody who can unlock it early, not even us.

  1. You choose the wait

    01

    When you set it up, you choose how long getting out should take, anywhere from a week to a year, and you can always make it longer.

  2. Leaving starts a countdown

    02

    If you ever ask to leave, the countdown starts. When it ends, you’re free to go.

  3. Stay, and it never happened

    03

    Change your mind, press Stay, and it’s as if you never asked.

The only shortcut is a friend you choose, who can bring the wait down to a day but can never stop you leaving.

Going all in? We’re working on a Hardcore lock, where even wiping your Mac won’t free it before your wait is over.

The lock is being built now. See where things stand.

Why it exists

Pornography is the most patient thing ever built. It’s free, it never closes, it never runs out, and every inch of it was designed by people who get paid when you stay. It doesn’t have to win the argument. It only has to wait until you’re tired, until it’s late, until the house is quiet and the part of you that knows better has gone to sleep. Then it takes a little more of the person you were trying to become.

Willpower alone rarely beats that, because willpower is the first thing to leave at two in the morning. So SafeSight Mac moves the decision. You make it in daylight, clear-eyed, knowing what you want your life to look like, and it keeps that promise on the nights you can’t. The person you are on your best day gets the final word. The worst hour of your week doesn’t get a vote.

Nobody is watching over your shoulder, nothing is reported to anyone, and there’s no lecture. Wanting this isn’t a confession. It’s the same instinct that makes someone leave their card at home before a night out, or pour the bottle down the sink while they still can. You’re locking a door from the inside. The key is still yours; you’ve just made the walk to it long enough to remember why you locked it.

This is about freedom. Not the thin kind, where you do whatever the next five minutes want, but the real kind: being the same person at midnight that you were at noon.

The usual tricks

Hard to get around, by design

Most blockers fall to a five-minute trick, and everyone who’s needed one knows the tricks. SafeSight Mac is designed so the usual ones don’t work.

The usual ways around a blocker, and what happens with most blockers and with SafeSight Mac

Use an app instead of a browser Most blockersUsually not filtered SafeSight MacCovered in every app

Ask whoever holds the password Most blockersOne weak moment, one text SafeSight MacNobody holds a password. Only the friend you chose can shorten your wait, and only to a day. Not even we can

About that last row: wiping is the one way around it we haven’t closed yet, and we’re working on it. If you know macOS well and have ideas for making a wipe harder to use as an escape hatch, we’d love your help. Open an issue or start with CONTRIBUTING.md. If your idea involves a way around the lock, please send it privately through SECURITY.md instead.

The basics

How it works

Everything stays on your Mac.

There’s no account and no server, and your screen is checked on the device itself. Nothing you see is ever uploaded.

It’s free forever.

No trial, no card, no account. The core will never be put behind a paywall.

What the core includes

The code is public.

The lock is designed to hold even though anyone can read every line.

Read it on GitHub

What it can’t do

  • Wiping your Mac removes it.
  • It can miss an image, and it sometimes covers a harmless one.
  • It only protects this Mac, not your phone, a TV or another computer.
