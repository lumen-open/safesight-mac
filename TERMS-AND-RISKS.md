# Terms and risks

**Version 0.1, draft, 19 November 2026.** This version covers the early preview, which has the screen cover but not the lock yet. It hasn't been read by a lawyer.

SafeSight Mac is free, open-source software. Please read this short page before you install it.

## Who it's for

- **For yourself, or for your child.** Set it up for yourself, or, if you're a parent or legal guardian, for your child on a Mac you own. Never set it up for another adult, and never make it a condition of a job, a home, a membership or anyone's support.
- **A Mac you own.** Install it only on a Mac you own and control, not a work or school Mac. On a shared family Mac, it protects the accounts it's set up on.
- **Who holds the password matters.** If someone else controls your password, they control your Mac, and no software can change that.

## What it is, and what it isn't

SafeSight Mac covers explicit images and video on your screen. It's a tool, not a treatment for anything, and it doesn't replace the people in your life or professional help.

## What can go wrong

- It can miss an explicit image, and it sometimes covers a harmless one. How many it misses hasn't been measured yet.
- An image can show for a moment before its cover appears.
- Protected video, like films in streaming apps, can't be checked.
- It only protects this Mac, not your phone, a TV or another computer.
- This preview has no lock, so you can pause or quit it at any time.
- It's an early release built with the help of AI, and nobody independent has reviewed it yet. It may have bugs, so keep a backup of your Mac.

The full list of known limits is in [docs/STEP-1-STATUS.md](docs/STEP-1-STATUS.md).

There's no support line. Bugs go in the public issue tracker, and anything that could help someone get around SafeSight Mac goes privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
