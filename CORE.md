# The free-forever core

These parts are free forever. They will never be put behind a paywall, time-limited or reduced to make a paid tier look better:

- the screen cover, on every display the Mac draws, with the detector;
- the lock: the keeper, the chosen wait, Leave and Stay, the Recovery Key;
- the optional keyholder;
- updates, including security fixes.

There is no account, no trial and no card. If a paid tier ever exists, it is for extras only, and payment can never weaken or unlock anything: a lapsed or cancelled payment leaves the free core exactly as it was.
