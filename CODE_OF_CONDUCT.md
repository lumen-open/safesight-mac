# Code of conduct

SafeSight Mac exists to help people get their freedom back. Many people who use it, or who help build it, are in the middle of something hard. Treat everyone here the way you'd want to be treated on your worst day.

## What we expect

- **Kindness and respect,** in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- **No shame.** Nobody here is judged for why they need this tool, and nobody asks.
- **Privacy.** Never post anyone's personal details, and never ask people what they struggle with.
- **No explicit material, ever,** anywhere in the project: not in issues, tests, screenshots, examples or links. Use synthetic test patterns.
- **No ways around the lock in public.** If you find one, report it privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Posts describing a way around it are hidden and moved to a private report.
- **No pressure on people.** Nobody can shorten anyone's wait, including the maintainers. Don't ask, and don't push others to.

## What isn't okay

Harassment, insults, threats, sexual content or language, discrimination, trolling, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private report form on this repository ("Report a vulnerability" under the Security tab), with a title starting **"Conduct:"**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled discreetly.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or hide content, warn someone, or block them from the project, depending on what happened. We'll always try to be fair, and to act quickly when someone is being hurt.
