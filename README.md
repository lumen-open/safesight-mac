# Fernwick Open (working name)

**Why this exists:** so that men and women can experience freedom, and to fight back against a dark power that takes it from them.

Fernwick Open is a free-forever, open-source Mac app. You install it on your own Mac and choose how long switching it off will take: 7 days at least, up to a year. Explicit pictures are covered on your screens; everything else keeps working. Asking to leave starts a countdown, and pressing **Stay** throws the request away. An optional friend can shorten the wait to a day, but can never block it.

**Status: step 0.** Nothing works yet. This repository holds the project's foundations: licence, rules, build checks and a "hello" build. The cover-only app comes next; the lock after that.

## The honest promise

> Fernwick Open is free, open-source software, built mostly by AI and provided as is. Nobody independent has tested it. It turns your own Mac into one where explicit pictures are covered and where switching protection off takes a wait you chose in advance. There is no company and no server, and nobody can shorten your wait for you, including the people who made it. It is not unbeatable: erasing your Mac always ends it, a determined person can find ways around it, it misses some pictures and wrongly covers others, and it does nothing for your phone, a TV or another computer. It may have bugs that lock you out of parts of your own Mac, so keep a backup. It is for adults, on their own Mac, by their own choice. It is not treatment for anything.

This project never says "can't be bypassed", "100%", "blocks all", "treats addiction" or "nobody can ever unlock it".
